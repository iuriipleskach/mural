# Research Note: Mural `LiveTransport` & `ConversationCoordinator` Event Contract vs Parlo Turn Pipeline

**Ticket**: `iuriipleskach/mural#2` (Part of Map `#1`)  
**Date**: 2026-11-12  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Mural's `ConversationCoordinator` and `LiveTransport` implement a **continuous, full-duplex WebRTC session** (`gpt-live-1`, data channel `"oai-events"`, audio track `"mural-microphone"`) where speech recognition, conversational turn generation, and voice synthesis happen remotely inside a stateful Realtime session, while helper tasks (meaning subtitles, post-turn pedagogical assessment, typed-reply generation, web-search delegation, word lookups) execute out-of-band over HTTP (`POST /v1/responses`, model `"gpt-6-luna"`).

Parlo (`Packages/SpeechKit`, `Packages/ConversationEngine`, `Packages/ParloKit`) implements a **discrete, half-duplex, zero-egress on-device turn loop**:
1. **ASR (`AsrService` / `LiveAsrService`)**: Push-to-talk (`startRecording()` → `stopRecording() -> VerbatimTranscript`) using `AVAudioEngine` + `SFSpeechRecognizer` (`requiresOnDeviceRecognition = true`) with background IEEE-float WAV capture (`UtteranceCache`).
2. **Engine (`ConversationEngine`)**: Deterministic token budgeting (`TokenBudget.afmDefault`: 4,096 window / 3,200 call budget / 640 reserve) → `TranscriptCondenser` → `FoundationModelProvider` (`LanguageModelSession.respond(to:generating: AFMRoleplayTurn.self)`) → `CorrectionGate` (`GatedCorrection`) → `ConversationStoring`.
3. **TTS (`TtsService` / `SystemTtsService`)**: `AVSpeechSynthesizer` speaking `RoleplayTurn.reply`.

Because `ConversationCoordinator` couples to `LiveTransport` through **four closure callbacks** (`onEvent`, `onLevels`, `onFailure`, `onHostedLease`) and **five imperative methods** (`connect`, `send`, `mute`, `close`, `disconnect`), bridging or replacing `LiveTransport` with Parlo's on-device pipeline requires translating between Mural's **event-stream + out-of-band instruction injection** paradigm and Parlo's **request/response turn + structured `@Generable` output** paradigm.

## 3. Mapping Mural's Runtime Contract onto Parlo's Turn Pipeline

### 3.1 Architecture & Data-Flow Comparison

## 4. Audio Level Callbacks & `AVAudioSession` Lifecycle Comparison

### 4.1 Audio Level Metering (`onLevels`)
* **Mural (`LiveTransport.swift:169-190`)**: Polls `RTCPeerConnection.statistics` every 100 ms on a background task, reads `"media-source"` (`input`) and `"inbound-rtp"` (`output`) `audioLevel` (`0.0...1.0`), applies EMA smoothing (`0.35 * prev + 0.65 * min(1, level * 4)`), and invokes `onLevels?(isMuted ? 0 : lastInput, lastOutput)` on `@MainActor`.
  * `ConversationCoordinator` uses `inputLevel` and `outputLevel` for two things:
    1. **UI Orb & Status (`RootView.swift:199`, `ConversationCoordinator.swift:162`)**: `MuralOrb(energy: max(outputLevel, inputLevel * 0.45), ...)` and status text (`outputLevel > 0.02 ? "Mural is speaking" : inputLevel > 0.02 ? "I’m listening" : "Take your time"`).
    2. **Inactivity Grace (`ConversationCoordinator.swift:137-140`, `ConversationActivity.swift:34-38`)**: `input > 0.03` calls `activity.inputActive(now:)`, granting a 15s `speechGraceSeconds` extension on silence timeouts; `output > 0.03` calls `activity.assistantActive(now:)`.
* **Parlo (`LiveAsrService.swift:126-141`, `TtsService.swift:41-60`)**:
  * `LiveAsrService.installInputTap` already receives `AVAudioPCMBuffer` (`buffer.floatChannelData`, `frameLength: 1024`) on bus 0 (`LiveAsrService.swift:132-140`), where RMS/peak `inputLevel` can be computed trivially alongside `continuation.yield(data)`.
  * `SystemTtsService` (`TtsService.swift:41-60`) uses `AVSpeechSynthesizer` without metering or delegate callbacks. To drive `outputLevel` in `ConversationCoordinator`, `SystemTtsService` must adopt `AVSpeechSynthesizerDelegate` (`didStart`, `willSpeakRangeOfSpeechString`, `didFinish`, `didCancel`) or generate buffers via `AVSpeechSynthesizer.write(_:toBufferCallback:)` / synthetic speech envelope while `isSpeaking` is true.

### 4.2 `AVAudioSession` Lifecycle & Half-Duplex vs Full-Duplex Contention
* **Mural (`LiveTransport.swift:53-65`, `157-161`)**:
  * Activates `RTCAudioSession` **once** at `connect()` with category `.playAndRecord`, mode `.voiceChat`, options `[.defaultToSpeaker, .allowBluetoothHFP]`, and keeps the hardware voice-processing I/O unit active for the entire multi-minute call until `disconnect()` sets `setActive(false)`.
  * Because `.voiceChat` runs hardware acoustic echo cancellation (AEC) inside WebRTC's voice processing audio unit, microphone capture and speaker output run simultaneously without feedback.
* **Parlo (`LiveAsrService.swift:271-284`, `TtsService.swift:41-60`, `ConversationViewModel.swift:125`)**:
  * `LiveAsrService.configureAudioSession()` sets `.playAndRecord`, mode `.spokenAudio`, options `[.defaultToSpeaker, .allowBluetoothHFP]` and `setActive(true, options: .notifyOthersOnDeactivation)` on **every** `startRecording()`, and calls `setActive(false, options: .notifyOthersOnDeactivation)` on **every** `stopRecording()` / `cancelRecording()` (`LiveAsrService.swift:188, 232, 271-284`).
  * **Critical Lifecycle Conflict**: Calling `AVAudioSession.sharedInstance().setActive(false)` inside `LiveAsrService.stopRecording()` immediately before `SystemTtsService.speak(...)` can interrupt or silence `AVSpeechSynthesizer` or cause route flaps, and if `LiveAsrService` and `AVSpeechSynthesizer` ever overlap, the mic will transcribe the synthesizer's output.
  * **Resolution**:
    1. Keep `AVAudioSession` active in `.playAndRecord` (`.spokenAudio` or `.voiceChat` with `[.defaultToSpeaker, .allowBluetoothHFP]`) across the conversation session (matching Mural's session-scoped activation in `connect()` / `disconnect()`), rather than deactivating `AVAudioSession` at the end of every user utterance.
    2. Enforce strict **half-duplex turn handoff** between `LiveAsrService` and `SystemTtsService`:
       - When the user begins speaking (`startRecording`), call `await tts.stopSpeaking()` (`ConversationViewModel.swift:125`).
       - When `stopRecording()` completes and `ConversationEngine.submit(_:)` produces a reply, keep `LiveAsrService` in `.idle` (tap removed) while `SystemTtsService.speak(...)` plays, and await `AVSpeechSynthesizerDelegate.speechSynthesizer(_:didFinish:)` before re-enabling mic capture or clearing `outputLevel`.
    3. Preserve Mural's `AVAudioSession.interruptionNotification` observer (`ConversationCoordinator.swift:147-150`) to cleanly end/cancel active recording and TTS on phone calls or system interruptions.
